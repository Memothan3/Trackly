Frequently asked questions

How do I add my first transaction?
👤
✦
Sign in, open the dashboard, and use Add transaction — income, expense, or transfer. Balances update automatically.
Can I upload receipts?
👤
✦
Yes. Attach images or PDFs when logging expenses. AI extracts amount, merchant, and category in the classic dashboard.
Is my data secure?
👤
✦
Firebase authentication plus Supabase storage. Your data stays in your project — never sold to third parties.
